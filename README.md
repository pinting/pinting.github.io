## Assets & Credits

The pixel art character and world scenery used in the animation originate from Nintendo's **Super Mario Bros. 3** (NES, 1988):

- **Character:** Raccoon Mario (*Shippō Mario*) running animation
- **Scenery Assets:** Hills, ground, bushes, palm trees, and log platforms from World 1 (Grass Land), World 2 (Desert Land), and World 3 (Sea Side)
- **Original Developers & Artists (Nintendo R&D4 / EAD):**
  - **Takashi Tezuka**, **Hideki Konno**, **Hiroyuki Kimura** — Graphic Designers & Artists
